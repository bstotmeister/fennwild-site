Privacy Policy

Effective {{privacy_effective_date}}. Applies to {{game_name}} on Android, iOS and Steam, and to this website.

{{game_name}} is made by {{studio}} ("we"). We built the game to work without an account and to keep your data on your device. This policy explains the small amount of data that leaves it, why, and your choices.

What stays on your device

  • Save games and settings — stored only in the game's private storage on your device (or Steam's local user folder). We never receive them.
  • Gameplay statistics shown in menus (play time, kith bonded) — also local.

What we collect

We do not run analytics, crash reporting or accounts of our own. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Advertising (Android and iOS only)

The mobile versions show ads served by Google AdMob: banners on the title, shop and settings screens, occasional full-screen ads between zones, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK collects device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and OS version, app usage related to ads, and approximate location derived from the IP address. How Google uses this is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. On first launch, where required by law (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) you see a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform and can decline personalised ads; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the game. On iOS, the system asks whether the app may track you; declining limits ads to non-personalised ones. The game plays exactly the same either way.

Remove Ads. Buying the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase stops all banners and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain available but only if you choose to watch one.

Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms; we receive no payment details. The game stores only a flag that the purchase was made, and re-checks it with the store when you reinstall or tap Restore purchases. On Steam the game is a paid download with no ads and no in-app purchases.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operational purposes under GitHub's privacy statement. We set no cookies and use no analytics here. The play-test sign-up links to Google Groups and Google Play, which are governed by Google's policies.

Children

{{game_name}} is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We tag ad requests to request content rated for teens and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share any beyond the advertising and store providers named above, who process it for their own purposes as described in their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, these requests concern Google (AdMob), Apple or Valve; you can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings, withdraw ad consent in the game's settings, or delete the game, which removes all local data. We are happy to help you work out where to send a request.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note it in the game's release notes.
