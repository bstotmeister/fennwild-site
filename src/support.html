When reporting a bug, include your platform (Android / iOS / Steam), the game version from Settings → About, what you did and what happened. A screenshot or short video helps a lot.

Frequently asked

I bought Remove Ads but ads are back.

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought with. The game re-checks the store and removes the ads again. This is also how to carry the purchase to a new device.

Can I decline personalised ads?

Yes. The consent form shows on first launch where the law requires it, and Settings → Privacy options lets you change your answer later. On iOS you can also deny tracking in the system prompt or in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

Where is my save?

Saves live on your device only. Deleting the game deletes them; there is no cloud save yet. Steam Cloud support is planned for a later update.

The fight is too hard / too easy.

Enemy wind-ups are telegraphed by a flash; dodge during the flash to pass through the attack with no damage. Aspects matter: each of the eight beats two others and loses to two. If you lose, you wake at the last lantern with your kith healed and 10 % less money — nothing else is lost. On mobile you can also watch a rewarded ad to revive your party on the spot.

Controller support?

Steam: yes, any controller Steam Input recognises, plus keyboard and mouse. Mobile: Bluetooth controllers work; the touch overlay hides when one is connected.

Accessibility

Screen shake can be turned off, aspect icons carry shapes as well as colours, the touch layout can be scaled and made more opaque, and every control can be remapped on Steam.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Google Play, Apple, Steam. We cannot issue refunds directly.
