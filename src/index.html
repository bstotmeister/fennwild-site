Every encounter is a fight where you stand

No battle screen

Walk into tall grass and a wild kith rises. The camera locks on the spot, you step aside and take direct control of the kith at your heel.

Four abilities and a dodge

Real-time combat with cooldowns, telegraphed attacks and invulnerable dodge frames. Read the flash, roll through it, punish.

Bond, swap, grow

Weaken a wild kith and throw a bond charm. Swap mid-fight. Eight aspects, each beating two and beaten by two.

Five zones, four Wardens

Reedmarsh, Thornbrake, Emberscar, Glasshollow and the Sky Moors, ending in the Heartfen. A compact 5–8 hour adventure.
